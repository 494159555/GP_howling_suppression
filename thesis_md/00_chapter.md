# 摘要

在室内使用扩音系统时，声反馈现象会导致音频在某些频率点出现正反馈，引发啸叫。现有的啸叫抑制技术主要依赖移频、陷波抑制和自适应反馈消除等传统算法方案，这些方案在抑制啸叫的同时往往会损害语音质量。近年来，深度学习技术在音频处理领域取得了显著进展，为啸叫抑制提供了新的解决途径。本文将啸叫抑制问题抽象为频域掩膜估计任务，提出了一种基于U-Net卷积神经网络的啸叫抑制方法，通过学习带啸叫频谱到干净频谱的映射关系，实现端到端的啸叫抑制。本文设计了从基础到高级的多个U-Net模型变体，包括3层基线U-Net、5层标准U-Net、引入注意力机制的U-Net以及集成残差连接与空洞卷积的综合优化U-Net，系统性地探索不同网络设计对啸叫抑制效果的影响。同时，本文实现了移频法、增益抑制法和自适应反馈消除法三种传统方法作为基线进行对比。实验从模型架构、损失函数、训练策略和数据增强等多个维度进行了系统的对比分析。结果表明，综合优化模型取得了最优性能（SI-SDR为11.41dB，PESQ为2.39，STOI为0.95），在各项客观评价指标上均显著优于传统方法（SI-SDR均为负值）。消融实验表明，残差连接是性能提升的主要贡献组件（单独添加后SI-SDR提升0.46dB），注意力机制和空洞卷积在当前模型规模下的边际贡献有限，但三者的集成仍具有整体协同价值。本研究验证了深度学习方法应用于啸叫抑制的可行性和有效性。

关键词：啸叫抑制；声反馈；U-Net；注意力机制；深度学习

## Abstract

When using a public address system indoors, acoustic feedback can cause positive feedback at certain frequencies, resulting in howling. Existing howling suppression techniques mainly rely on traditional approaches such as frequency shifting, notch filtering, and adaptive feedback cancellation, which often degrade signal quality while suppressing howling. In recent years, deep learning has made significant progress in audio processing, offering a new approach to howling suppression. This paper formulates the howling suppression problem as a spectral mask estimation task and proposes a U-Net convolutional neural network based method, achieving end-to-end suppression by learning the mapping from howling-corrupted spectrograms to clean spectrograms. Multiple U-Net model variants are designed, including a 3-layer baseline, a 5-layer standard U-Net, a U-Net with attention mechanisms, and a comprehensively optimized U-Net integrating residual connections and atrous convolutions, systematically investigating the impact of different network designs on howling suppression performance. Three traditional methods (frequency shifting, gain suppression, and adaptive feedback cancellation) are also implemented as baselines. Comprehensive experiments compare multiple dimensions including model architecture, loss functions, training strategies, and data augmentation. Results show that the optimized model achieves the best performance (SI-SDR of 11.41 dB, PESQ of 2.39, STOI of 0.95), significantly outperforming all traditional methods (all with negative SI-SDR). Ablation studies with three random seeds reveal that residual connections are the primary contributing component (SI-SDR improvement of 0.46 dB when added alone), while attention mechanisms and atrous convolutions show limited marginal benefits at the current model scale, though their integration still provides synergistic value.

Keywords: Howling Suppression; Acoustic Howling; U-Net; Attention Mechanism; Deep Learning
